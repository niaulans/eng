### Sentence word order

#### The basic word order of an English sentence

```
The meaning of an English sentence depends on the word order.
1. We put the subject before the verb and the object after the verb:
-> The cook | burnt | the dinner.

2. Adverbials (How?, Where?, When?) usually come after the verb or after the object:
-> He read the note quickly. (How?) I waited at the corner (Where?) till 11.30. (When?)

3. The basic word order of a sentence that is not a question or a command is usually:
-> subject | verb | object | adverbials (How? When? Where?)
   I (S) bought (V) a hat (O)
   The children (S) have gone (V) home (Adv)
   We (S) ate (V) our meal (O) in silence (Adv)

4. We also put the time reference at the beginning: Yesterday I bought a hat
```
